> Thirteen short games where you spend a **realistic day in a real job**. Five take place at Meridian, a software company. Four are at an ad agency across town. Three are at Hallam Works, an engineering company that builds real equipment. And one is at a consulting firm, helping another company solve its problem. ## 9. First-run help tips

These appear the first time you start a day.

1. **Read the line above.** It tells you who to find and which room they're in. The map next to it shows where you are. Tap any room to walk there.
2. **Then answer in the box below the office.** Nothing happens until you tap one of the answers. That's how the day begins.

**Button:** Got it

## 13. Jargon check (pops up mid-conversation)

The game rotates through these three questions:
1. Quick question before I go on. Do you know what **[term]** means?
2. Wait a second. Do you know what **[term]** means?
3. Before I keep going, do you know what **[term]** means?

**Answer buttons:** Yes, I know that one · No, please explain

**After the explanation:** Got it, keep going
