Nine in the morning · day one

Chronicles Two workplaces, one deadline, and the decisions the job is actually made of.

Nine short games where you work a real day in a real job. Five of them at Meridian, a product company; four at the agency across town. Walk the floor, talk to the people who have already made up their minds, and make the calls yourself. Everything closes at five, whether you are finished or not.

  • 20–40 min a run
  • No install — opens in the browser
  • Phone or desktop
  • Ends with what you actually learned

How a run goes

Nine o'clock to five, in about half an hour

  • Take the brief

    You arrive on your first day into a problem already in progress. Somebody hands you the vague version of it.

  • Work the floor

    Move between rooms and talk to the people who hold the pieces. Their theories conflict. That is the job.

  • Make the call

    Every choice lands somewhere. At five you get the certificate, the portfolio, and the lessons the day was really about.

Chronicles — short games about doing the work. Best with sound off in an open-plan office.
